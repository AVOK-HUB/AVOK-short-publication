AAROK Short Publication

Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 15, 2026
Last updated: May 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AAROK Short Publication handles information. AAROK Short Publication is an internal application operated by AAROK to prepare and manually publish original gaming clips to the AAROK Pinterest account.

Application operator

The application is operated by AAROK for creator and brand use. It is not a public social network, advertising network, marketplace, or third-party user platform.

Information processed

The application may process the following information when provided or authorized by AAROK:

  • Original gaming video files selected by AAROK.
  • Generated cover images for those videos.
  • Pin titles, descriptions, links, hashtags, board names, and board IDs.
  • Pinterest authorization information required to publish to AAROK's own Pinterest account.

How information is used

Information is used only to prepare, preview, and manually publish AAROK's own content to Pinterest. The application does not sell personal information, does not provide data to advertisers, and does not use Pinterest data for unrelated tracking or profiling.

Manual publication

Each Pin is reviewed and triggered manually by AAROK. The application is not designed for spam, mass automated publishing, or publishing content from other Pinterest users.

Data sharing

Content and metadata are sent to Pinterest only when AAROK chooses to publish a Pin through the Pinterest API. Content published to Pinterest is also subject to Pinterest's own terms and privacy practices.

Data storage

Drafts may be stored locally in AAROK's browser. API credentials, when configured, are intended to be stored in a private server environment and are not intentionally made public.

Data retention

Local drafts may be deleted by AAROK at any time from the application environment. Published Pinterest content can be managed through the AAROK Pinterest account.

Contact

For privacy questions, data deletion requests, or questions about AAROK Short Publication, contact the application operator by email: KALIMPERA@protonmail.com.
